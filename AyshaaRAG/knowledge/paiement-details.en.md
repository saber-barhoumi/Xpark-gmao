## Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (most common for companies), cheque and cash within legal
limits. Bank details appear on every invoice. Online card payment is planned
in upcoming website evolutions — meanwhile, bank transfer is the simplest.

## When do I have to pay?

According to agreed terms: on delivery for occasional clients, or by invoice
due date (shown on each invoice) for account clients. Once payment is
received, the invoice switches to "Paid" in Client Area → Finances — nothing
to do on your side.

## Is VAT included in the prices?

Estimates shown at booking are service amounts; the invoice details the
amount excluding tax, VAT (19%, current Tunisian rate) and the total
including tax. VAT-registered companies recover VAT normally — our invoice
carries all required mentions.

## Do you offer monthly billing for regular clients?

Yes. Clients with recurring transports can move to an account with grouped
billing (e.g. monthly) and terms agreed with our billing department. Ask via
a Support ticket, category "Billing", or call +216 70 000 000.

## What happens if I pay late?

Best is to tell us before the due date: we'll find an arrangement. Unpaid
invoices remain visible in your area with their status, and our billing team
follows up. A prolonged silence may suspend new bookings — we'd rather not,
so let's talk 😊.

## Can I get a duplicate invoice?

No need to ask: all your invoices remain downloadable as PDF anytime from
Client Area → Documents, with the logo and official mentions. Lost access to
your account? Support restores it quickly.
