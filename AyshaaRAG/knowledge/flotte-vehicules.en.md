## What types of vehicles do you have?

Our fleet covers three families: **light utility vehicles** (up to ~800 kg —
parcels, fast urban deliveries), **vans** (1 to 1.5 tonnes — pallets,
equipment, our most requested format) and **trucks** (3.5 tonnes and above —
large volumes, industrial transport). All are recent, maintained under a
strict plan and monitored in real time.

## Which vehicle should I choose for my goods?

You don't have to choose! When booking, you enter the weight and type of
goods: our system automatically selects the most suitable (and most
economical) vehicle. Our pricing AI includes the vehicle type in the
calculation — you immediately see the price difference. For a very specific
need, call +216 70 000 000 and an advisor will guide you.

## Up to what weight can you transport?

Our trucks handle loads up to several tonnes. Beyond that, or for oversized
goods, we study the request case by case with the Custom transport service —
there's always a solution, possibly in several rotations.

## Are your vehicles insured and inspected?

Yes. Every vehicle has up-to-date insurance, scheduled technical inspections
and a maintenance logbook managed by our technicians. Before each departure,
a checklist is verified: vehicle documents, fuel voucher, highway card if
needed. The safety of your goods starts with impeccable vehicles.

## Can I follow the vehicle during transport?

Yes — from Client Area → Live tracking you see your mission's progress in
real time: driver assigned, departure, en route, estimated arrival. The page
refreshes automatically every 30 seconds.
