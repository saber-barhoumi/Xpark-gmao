## Are my goods insured during transport?

Yes. Every XPARK transport is covered by our goods insurance throughout the
service, from pickup to delivery. Vehicles carry up-to-date insurance and
drivers are trained in handling. For high-value goods, mention it at
booking: we adapt the coverage and precautions.

## How are fragile goods protected?

Mention "fragile" in your booking description: the load is secured and
wedged accordingly, and the driver sees it on his mission order. Good
original packaging remains your best protection — if unsure about proper
packaging, ask the team for advice.

## What should I do in case of damage or delay?

Open a ticket from Client Area → Support, category "Mission", linking the
mission concerned — it's the fastest channel, your file reaches the team
with full context. Describe the problem (photos welcome by email to
contact@xpark.tn as a complement). The team handles every claim and replies
directly in your area. Remember to note reservations at delivery if you see
a problem.

## Are there goods you refuse?

Yes, for everyone's safety: regulated dangerous or flammable materials
(fuels, explosives, unpackaged chemicals), illegal goods, and generally
anything requiring a special license that general freight doesn't cover.
If in doubt about your goods, ask me or call +216 70 000 000 — better to
check before booking.

## Who loads and unloads the goods?

By default, the sender prepares the goods at departure and the recipient
receives at arrival; the driver supervises securing the load in the vehicle.
If you need handling help, mention it in your transport request — we'll
organize it with you.
