## How does a mission work for a driver?

The driver checks his missions in the XPARK GMAO backoffice. Before
departure, a logistics checklist is verified: fuel voucher granted (with its
number), vehicle documents check, highway card handed over if the route
requires it. During the mission, the status moves from "pending" to
"in progress" then "completed" at closure, with departure and return mileage
recorded. The client follows this progress in real time from their area.

## Who assigns missions to drivers?

Client requests arrive as "pending assignment". A driver and a vehicle are
assigned by managers from the backoffice, or proposed automatically by the
OptimisateurFlotteIA — the optimization module that distributes missions
according to vehicle availability, distances and costs.

## What are the roles in XPARK GMAO?

- **ADMIN / ADMINISTRATOR**: full access, user management.
- **MANAGER (GESTIONNAIRE)**: missions, assignments, invoices, analytics.
- **DRIVER (CHAUFFEUR)**: own missions, logistics checklist, operational statuses.
- **TECHNICIAN**: maintenance, interventions, fleet condition.
- **CLIENT**: website client area (booking, tracking, invoices, support).
Financial analytics are restricted to management: a driver only sees
operational indicators, never any amounts.

## How is vehicle maintenance managed?

Each vehicle follows a preventive maintenance plan (technical inspections,
scheduled servicing) plus curative interventions in case of breakdown,
managed by technicians in the Maintenance module: interventions, work orders
and spare parts. Mileage tracking and regular checks guarantee fleet
availability and safety.

## How is fuel tracked?

Every refill is recorded (date, quantity, price, mileage, station, source)
and linked to its vehicle. Management monitors average consumption
(L/100 km), monthly cost evolution and the ranking of vehicles by fuel cost
in the backoffice Analytics page.
