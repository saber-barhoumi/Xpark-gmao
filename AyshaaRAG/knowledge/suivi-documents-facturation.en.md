## How do I track my delivery live?

In Client Area → Live tracking, every active mission shows its real-time
progress: request confirmed → driver assigned → on the road → delivered. You
can see the driver, the vehicle, the distance and the estimated arrival time.
The page refreshes automatically every 30 seconds. Key updates also appear in
the notifications 🔔 of your client area.

## Where can I find my invoices?

Client Area → Finances: all your invoices (number, date, due date, total
amount, paid/unpaid status), your total spending, ongoing commitments and a
cost summary per mission. Every invoice can be downloaded as PDF from the
Documents tab.

## Which documents can I download?

Client Area → Documents gathers, automatically generated from your data: the
**mission order** (for each confirmed transport), the **transport
certificate** (for each completed mission) and your **invoices**. Each
document opens in a printable view with the XPARK logo: choose "Save as PDF"
to download it. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the XPARK website.

## How do I pay an invoice?

Payment methods (bank transfer, cash, cheque) are indicated on the invoice
and agreed with our billing department. Once payment is received, the invoice
switches to "Paid" in your area. For any billing question, open a Support
ticket with the "Billing" category or write to contact@xpark.tn.

## Where can I see my transport statistics?

Client Area → Analytics: missions per month, kilometers traveled, total and
monthly spending, average cost per kilometer and your most frequent routes —
all computed live from your real missions.
