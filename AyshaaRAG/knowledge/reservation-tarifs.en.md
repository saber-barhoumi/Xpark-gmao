## How do I book a transport?

Booking is done online from the Client Area: log in on the XPARK website then
go to Client Area → Book. Choose the departure and arrival cities, the date,
the vehicle type and the weight of the goods. XPARK's artificial intelligence
instantly computes the distance, estimated duration and price. Confirm: your
request is registered and our team assigns a driver and a suitable vehicle.

If you don't have an account yet, click Register: it's free, and a
confirmation email with a code is sent to you.

## How much does a transport cost? How are prices calculated?

Prices depend on the distance, vehicle type, weight of the goods and the
highway option. XPARK uses a pricing artificial intelligence trained on
thousands of real trips: it instantly estimates the cost in Tunisian dinars
(TND) for any pair of cities. Example: a Tunis → Sfax van transport costs
about 150–200 TND depending on the load. For an exact price, start a booking
or request a free quote — the estimate is instant and without commitment.

## How do I request a quote?

Three ways: 1) the Contact page form (name, company, email, phone, transport
request); 2) Client Area → Book, which gives an instant estimated price;
3) by phone at +216 70 000 000. Our team studies every request and replies
as quickly as possible.

## Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. As long as the mission is "pending" (no driver assigned yet), you can
cancel it yourself from Client Area → My missions → Cancel button. Once the
mission is assigned or in progress, contact support (Client Area → Support)
or call +216 70 000 000.

## What are the delivery times?

Delivery times depend on the route: they are estimated at booking time
(distance and duration computed by the AI, average speed around 60 km/h) and
agreed with you when the quote is validated. XPARK commits to respecting
them. Example: Tunis → Sfax is about 270 km, i.e. 3.5 to 4 hours of driving.

## Can I set up recurring transports?

Yes. The Custom transport service lets you plan regular routes adapted to
your business, with a dedicated contact person. Describe your need via the
Contact page or support, and our team builds the solution with you
(frequency, routes, vehicle type).
