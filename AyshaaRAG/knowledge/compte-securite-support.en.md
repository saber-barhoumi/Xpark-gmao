## How do I create a client account?

On the XPARK website, click Client Area → Register. Enter your first name,
last name, email and password (minimum 8 characters). A confirmation email
with a code is sent to you — the code expires after 24 hours. Once your email
is confirmed and the account approved, you receive a welcome email and can
log in.

## I forgot my password, what should I do?

On the login page, click "Forgot password". Enter your email: you receive a
reset code valid for 1 hour. Enter this code together with your new password
on the reset page. If the code has expired, simply request a new one.

## How do I secure my account (MFA)?

Client Area → Security offers two-step verification (MFA) with two methods:
1) **Authenticator app** (Google/Microsoft Authenticator) — scan a QR code,
then use a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds, the safest method;
2) **Email verification** — a 6-digit code sent at every login, valid
10 minutes with a maximum of 5 attempts. You can enable or disable each
method from the Security page and review your login history.

## How do I contact XPARK support?

Three channels: 1) **Client Area → Support**: open a ticket (subject,
category Mission / Billing / Technical / Other, optional linked mission,
message) — our team's reply appears directly in your area and you can close
the ticket once resolved; 2) **phone**: +216 70 000 000; 3) **email**:
contact@xpark.tn. Address: Industrial zone, Tunis, Tunisia.

## What are the support ticket statuses?

A ticket goes through: Open (created) → In progress (handled by the team) →
Resolved (answer provided) → Closed (closed by you or the team). You can
close your ticket yourself once the answer suits you.
