## Who is XPARK?

XPARK is a Tunisian company specialized in freight transport. It provides
professionals with a fleet of vehicles suited to every type of load: vans,
trucks and utility vehicles. XPARK delivers goods safely and on time,
anywhere in Tunisia.

Mission: provide reliable, secure transport services tailored to each
client's needs. Values: reliability, safety, punctuality, professionalism
and customer satisfaction.

## What services does XPARK offer?

XPARK offers five main services:
1. **Freight transport**: local and regional transport, secure delivery,
   on-time commitment, adapted to each client's needs.
2. **Fleet management**: vehicles suited to every mission, regular
   maintenance, maximum operational availability.
3. **Delivery and distribution**: shipment tracking from pickup to handover,
   route organization and trip optimization.
4. **Custom transport**: needs analysis, tailor-made planning, and a
   dedicated contact person throughout the service.
5. **Local and regional transport**: fast urban deliveries and regular
   inter-regional routes across Tunisia.

## Which areas does XPARK cover?

XPARK operates across all of Tunisia: urban deliveries (Tunis and major
cities) and connections between all regions. Routes are planned and optimized
by an artificial intelligence that computes distances, durations and costs
for every pair of Tunisian cities.

## What kind of goods do you transport?

XPARK transports all types of general goods: pallets, parcels, equipment and
professional material. Each request is analyzed to assign the vehicle best
suited to the volume and weight of the load. Goods are handled with care and
transported safely.

## Why choose XPARK?

- A modern fleet, regularly maintained and monitored in real time.
- Professional, experienced drivers.
- Instant AI-powered price estimates.
- A complete online client area: booking, live tracking, invoices,
  documents, analytics and support.
- Respected deadlines and follow-up of every operation.
